description=Sync and transfer projects between GEOX (xplus-studio.com/geox) and QGIS - import/export .geoxjson project bundles with styles, plus live push/pull over a local bridge.
about=GEOX is a browser GIS for urban analysis (networks, isochrones, isovists, space syntax). This plugin moves whole projects both ways: import .geoxjson bundles as styled QGIS layers, export QGIS layers back to GEOX with graduated/categorized symbology preserved, or start the live bridge and push/pull directly from an open GEOX tab. All data is WGS84 GeoJSON; the plugin reprojects at the QGIS edge.
